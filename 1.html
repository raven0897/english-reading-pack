② 可猜词 6 · 先猜再展开
超纲但能靠词根、合成、上下文推出来——先猜 10 秒，猜不出再展开。
lifestyle合成词：life（生活）+ style（样式）
本文义：生活方式。两个熟词拼一起，意思基本就是两义相加。
differ你认识 different / difference，去掉形容词后缀呢？
本文义：相异；不同。different 是「不同的」，differ 是动词「不同」。
cottage上下文：rural village 山村里的 simple ___，人住在里面
本文义：小屋；村舍。推不出精确义没关系，知道是「简朴的住处」就够读懂。
subway词根：sub-（在下）+ way（路）→ 什么路在下面？
本文义：地铁。sub- 家族还有 submarine（潜水艇，在海底的）。
extend词根：ex-（向外）+ tend（伸展）
本文义：延伸；扩展。Lifestyle choices also extend to diet. 也「延伸到」饮食。
diverse词根：di-（分开）+ verse（转）→ 各转向不同方向
本文义：多样的；丰富的。与 different 同源：转开 → 不一样。

原文

实线下划线＝核心单词｜浅底纹＝语块（整块记，别逐词拆）｜虚线＝可猜词（先猜）｜灰色小字＝认知词（直接看，不用记）｜初中新增词不再单独标注，义在上方自查区

People's lifestyles differ greatly around the world. These differences are shaped by many complex factors such as geography, history, economics（经济学）, and personal choice.

In busy cities like Shanghai or Beijing, people usually live life at a fast pace, with long working hours and constant online connection. Residents live in apartments, take crowded subways, and enjoy themselves in many restaurants, theaters and entertainment places.

In contrast, life in a rural village in the mountains may focus on agriculture（农业）, community, and a slower, more seasonal rhythm（节奏）. People usually live in simple cottages, and their daily life centers on family, neighbors and the land.

Lifestyle choices also extend to diet. Some adopt a vegetarian（素食的） diet for moral or health reasons, while others enjoy a diverse diet with local meats and vegetables. Leisure activities range from hiking in national parks to practicing yoga（瑜伽）, playing video games, or attending live music concerts.

Understanding these various（各种各样的） lifestyles helps us appreciate（欣赏；领会） the rich variety（多样性） of the human experience.

My City Week and My Village Weekend

I am a resident of Shanghai. My cousin lives in a rural village in the mountains, and our lifestyles differ greatly. My city life runs at a fast pace: every morning I take a crowded subway to work; every evening my phone keeps me in constant online connection with the office. On weekends, my leisure choices range from video games to live music concerts — the entertainment never stops.

In contrast, my cousin's life has a slower rhythm. He lives in a simple cottage, and his whole day centers on the farm and his family. His diet is simple: local vegetables and a little meat. He thinks my lifestyle is too complex. Maybe he is right. A good life does not need many factors — you just focus on family, health, and the community around you.

Choosing How We Live

Modern technology has quietly changed the pace of daily life. While our grandparents' days were shaped by the seasons, ours are shaped by screens: a constant flow of messages, entertainment and online activities leaves us little time for rest. For this reason, many people have begun to adopt a "slow" lifestyle. They focus on enjoying less rather than having more.

In contrast to those who fill every hour with leisure activities that range from attending live music concerts to playing video games, slow-life followers prefer diverse simple pleasures: a long walk, a home-cooked meal, a quiet evening with the community they love. Their choices even extend to diet — some adopt a vegetarian one for moral or health reasons — because they believe a good life does not depend on complex factors, but on the rhythm each of us chooses.
