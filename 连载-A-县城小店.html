第 1 集A · 县城小店 · 日记 · 2026-09-20 · 复习 12 词

Coming Back

Tuesday

I came back to the town where I grew up.

My grandfather's shop is on the corner of the main street. It is a small retail shop — tea, salt, oil, and a hundred small things. It has not opened for three months, and the key was still behind the door.

It is not a big city, and it is not really rural. It is a small town where everybody knows the name of your dog.

Inside, everything is where he left it. Every item has a price on it, written by hand. Most of the people who come here are local, and many of them are the same residents who came in twenty years ago.

Ten years ago this was the only shop in the town. Now there are three more, and one of them belongs to a big company from the city. Their prices are lower than ours. People still come to us, but they also buy on their phones now. For most consumers, price comes first. The things they buy are delivered to their door the next day, and they never have to leave home. That is convenience. Nobody can beat it, and the competition only gets harder.

My grandfather knew every person on this street. He knew who was ill, who had no money that week, who needed a bag of tea on Monday and would pay on Friday. He wrote it all in a small book. He did not care about the discount. He cared about the community.

I do not know if I can run a shop like that now. But this morning I opened the door, and a woman came in and asked for tea. She said she was glad to see the lights on.

So I am open. For now.

第 4 集A · 县城小店 · 微信聊天记录 · 2026-09-20 · 复习 13 词

The Group Chat

岸边小卖部 · 街坊群 · 18:42

Xiao Li: An, do you still have that dark tea? Two boxes.

An: Yes. I can deliver them to your door in ten minutes.

Xiao Li: Don't worry about it. I already made the purchase on my phone. Six yuan cheaper.

Xiao Li: [a page] The platform is paying part of the price this week.

Aunt Wang: Six yuan? Let me look through it.

Uncle Chen: I look through those pages every day and I still don't understand them. Last month I paid for a bag of rice and got nothing. Was that a scam?

An: Probably. Be careful, Uncle Chen.

Xiao Li: Sorry to say it here, An, but the impact on shops like yours is real.

Aunt Wang: Is the impact really that big?

An: It is. Our disadvantage is simple: they are big and we are small. I know that.

An: This whole street is undergoing a shift. My grandfather's shop underwent the same thing thirty years ago, when the first supermarket opened here. People walk in, look through the shop, and then purchase the same thing on their phones. Their behavior has changed, and it will not change back.

Aunt Wang: So what will you do?

An: I will not focus on price. I cannot win there. I will focus on one specific thing: I know this street.

An: I know who buys tea on Monday and pays on Friday. I know who is not well this week. I know which children come home at six. No phone knows that.

An: They can deliver to the whole country. In contrast, I can deliver to this street in ten minutes, and I can carry the rice to your door.

Uncle Chen: That is why I come.

Aunt Wang: Me too. Regardless of the price, I will still come to your shop. Some things are not about money.

Xiao Li: ...I feel a little bad now. I will take two boxes tomorrow. From you.

An: No need to feel bad. Just come when you need something.
