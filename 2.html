② 可猜词 8 · 先猜再展开
超纲但能靠词根、合成、上下文推出来——先猜 10 秒，猜不出再展开。
dramatic你认识 drama（戏剧），加 -tic 呢？
本文义：巨大的；戏剧性的。a dramatic revolution——从"戏剧性的"引申为"翻天覆地的、惊人的"。
unbelievableun-（不）+ believe（相信）+ -able（可…的）
本文义：难以置信的。unbelievable choice 多得让人不敢相信。
convenience你认识 convenient（方便的）——名词形式怎么变？
本文义：便利；方便。注意 convenient → convenience，拼写要去掉 -ent 换成 -ence。
shift上下文：This shift has created new opportunities —— 指代上文的什么？
本文义：转变；变化。This shift 回指前面说的"网购取代实体店"这件事。
competitioncompete（竞争）+ -ition（名词后缀）
本文义：竞争。increased competition 竞争加剧——竞争多了，价格才会低。
harmfulharm（伤害）+ -ful（充满…的）
本文义：有害的。harmful impact on the environment 对环境的有害影响。
disadvantagedis-（否定前缀）+ advantage（优势）
本文义：劣势；不利之处。disadvantage 就是 advantage 的反面。
behaviorbehave（表现）+ -ior（名词化，注意去掉词尾的 e）
本文义：行为；举止。consumer behavior 消费者行为。

原文

实线下划线＝核心单词｜浅底纹＝语块（整块记，别逐词拆）｜虚线＝可猜词（先猜）｜灰色小字＝认知词（直接看，不用记）｜初中新增词不再单独标注，义在上方自查区

The way we shop has undergone a dramatic revolution with the development of e-commerce（电子商务）.

Platforms（平台） like Taobao and JD.com have changed retail, offering consumers unbelievable choice and convenience.

Gone are the days when one needed to visit plenty of physical stores to find a specific item.

Now shoppers can click a few times, look through thousands of products, compare prices, read reviews, and have their purchases sent directly to their homes.

This shift has created new opportunities for businesses, allowing small sellers to reach a global market and deliver goods directly to customers, without the cost of running a physical store.

For consumers, the benefits are more than just convenience.

Online shopping often offers lower prices.

This is because of increased competition and people's ability to easily find different kinds of discounts and special offers.

However, this new situation also presents challenges.

There are serious problems, including online scams and privacy risks.

Packaging and delivery have a harmful impact on the environment.

Regardless of these disadvantages, online shopping has become a necessary part of modern life, greatly changing consumer behavior and the economy（经济）.

How I Shop Now

Gone are the days when I had to walk from shop to shop. Now I look through a shopping app, click a few times, and the things I want arrive at my door.

Last month I wanted a pair of shoes. The retail shop near my home asked for much more, but online I found the same item at a big discount. That shift in price was unbelievable to me, so I purchased them at once. Two days later they were delivered to my home — a small convenience I now never want to lose.

Regardless of the low price, I still check the shop's name first. Scams are real. I never give away my privacy, and I keep every purchase specific: I check the size and the color before I pay.

The Price We Do Not See

What has online shopping really undergone in twenty years? A revolution as dramatic as the one the printing press once brought. For the consumer, the global market is now a screen: a shop in another country is one click away, and opportunities that once belonged only to big retail shops now belong to anyone.

Yet the same way of shopping carries disadvantages we do not see. A price war means competition without end, and someone always pays for the low price. Bad goods, hidden costs and lost privacy are not accidents; they are part of the model. Harmful behavior — buying what we do not need — is exactly what discounts are made for. Regardless of how clever we think we are, the impact of every purchase reaches further than we can see.
