usersuse（使用）+ -r（做…的人）
本文义：用户。millions of users——使用这个平台的人。
endlessend（尽头）+ -less（没有…的）
本文义：无尽的；没完没了的。endless videos 一条接一条、没有尽头的视频。
creatorcreate（创造）+ -or（做…的人，注意去掉词尾的 e）
本文义：创作者。Anyone can become a creator 人人都能当创作者。
smartphonesmart（聪明的）+ phone（电话）
本文义：智能手机。合成词，两部分你都认识。
interactioninter-（相互）+ act（行动）+ -ion（名词后缀）
本文义：互动；交流。real-time interaction 实时互动。
increasinglyincrease（增加）+ -ing + -ly（副词后缀）
本文义：越来越多地。increasingly concerned about 越来越担心。
misinformationmis-（错误的）+ information（信息）
本文义：错误信息；假消息。the spread of misinformation 假消息的传播。
real-timereal（真实的）+ time（时间）
本文义：实时的。real-time interaction 实时互动——你发一条，对方马上看到。

原文

实线下划线＝核心单词｜浅底纹＝语块（整块记，别逐词拆）｜虚线＝可猜词（先猜）｜灰色小字＝认知词（直接看，不用记）｜初中新增词不再单独标注，义在上方自查区

In the past few years, short video platforms have exploded in popularity, fundamentally changing how we get information and entertainment.

Apps like Douyin have become a global phenomenon, attracting millions of users who spend hours watching endless videos.

These new forms offer unique creativity, humor, and fast communication and real-time interaction.

Anyone with a smartphone can become a creator, sharing their talent, knowledge, or daily life with a vast（广阔的；巨大的） audience.

From dance challenges to cooking tips（窍门；小贴士）, educational lectures（讲座） to travel vlogs（视频博客）, the variety is amazing.

However, this rapid rise also raises important questions.

People are increasingly concerned about addiction, the spread of misinformation and mental health impacts.

Critics（批评者） argue that these can reduce our ability to focus on longer, more complex tasks.

Despite these challenges, the influence of short video is undeniable（不可否认的）.

It has become a powerful force in shaping culture, trends, and public opinion in the digital age.

My Cousin's Videos

My cousin is a young creator on a short video platform. He makes every video with one smartphone — his cat, his lunch, the street outside his window. The variety surprises me: one day a dance, the next day a cooking tip.

At first only a few people watched him. Then one video attracted a big audience. Now he spends most of his free time doing one thing: answering his users in real-time. That interaction makes him happy, and his videos are increasingly long.

I am a little concerned about him. He says "five more minutes", and then he watches endless videos until late at night.

The Price of a Short Video

Few things have exploded as fast as short video. In five years it has grown from a small trend into a global phenomenon, and it has fundamentally changed what "boring" means: a two-minute wait now feels long.

This is not an accident. Every platform is built to attract attention and keep it — a unique face, a loud sound, one more video. The endless line of videos is the product, and we are what the product sells.

Critics have raised questions about what this does to us. Addiction is real, and so are the mental costs. Parents are concerned about what their children watch. Misinformation travels faster than the truth, because bad news is short and easy to share. Despite the fun, many users feel worse after an hour of it.

It is undeniable that short video is a powerful force. The question is not whether we use it, but whether we still choose when to stop.
